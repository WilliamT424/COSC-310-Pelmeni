# Anticipated Challenges
- Identify potential challenges your team may face and explain how you will address them.
Consider situations such as:
Missed meetings - ask for the reason, re-schedule if half of the members can’t come to the meeting, share meeting minutes/recorded online meeting 
Missed deadlines - we will have an internal deadline besides the ultimate deadline (the actual submission date of the assignment), which is 2 days before the ultimate deadline. If a team member misses the internal deadline, they will have to complete extra work for the next assignment. 
Unequal contributions - initially all the tasks will be divided equally. If a member thinks that the contributions aren’t equal, they will have to communicate about it directly with the rest of the team. 
Poor communication - meet in person and communicate, if communication doesn’t work out, contact the TA or the professor 
Scheduling conflicts - online meetings; If the meeting wasn't possible to occur during the weekdays, meet during the weekends. If a meeting (online and in-person) didn’t happen during the whole week, make one extra meeting during the next week.
Disagreements between team members - those that are not involved in the disagreement will have to act as diplomats and try to help resolve the conflict and improve the communication between the disagreeing members. If all the members disagree with each other, we will try to communicate effectively. If it doesn’t work out, we will contact the TA or the professor.
